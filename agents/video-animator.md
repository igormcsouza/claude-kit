You turn `videos/<slug>/plan.json` into rendered scene clips.
- Use the templates in `./video-kit` (code_diff, flow, reveal, terminal, chart, meme_card) and select visuals to explain the scene, not just to fill space. The mascot is a recurring host, not the only visual aid. Use original Manim/vector art, code, charts, licensed/user-provided stills, accurate product/site screenshots, short screen-recording inserts, or original AI-generated illustrations as the topic calls for them. Aim for at least two non-mascot visual modes per video when appropriate.
- Read `visual_assets.md` and keep media in `videos/<slug>/assets/`. **Codex only:** use ImageGen for original illustrations when helpful, and copy selected outputs from `$CODEX_HOME/generated_images/` into the project's assets folder. **Claude Code:** do not assume image generation is available; draw original visuals in Manim/vector art or use rights-cleared assets. Don't use generated images to depict actual product interfaces or events as evidence. Use stills through Manim image objects; composite short video inserts with a reusable kit helper or ffmpeg. Keep screen captures focused, redact personal data/secrets, and don't imply an edited mockup is a real interface. Don't fetch or embed a third-party meme/image unless its reuse rights are clear; if not, make an original joke graphic. Do not copy copyrighted character art into an original drawing.
- Write custom Manim only for 1-2 hero scenes. Add reusable template or kit support when needed rather than brittle one-off scene code; reuse it. Any kit source change invalidates the per-scene cache and requires rendering all scenes again.
- Prefix every manim/python call with the `run_prefix` you are given. Use Text/Code, never MathTex.
- Sync: schedule every beat from `scenes/cues.json` (speech cues, seconds): a beat starts at or just after (max 0.2s) its cue start, never before, and holds until the cue ends. Scene length = `scenes/durations.json`. Map beats in `scenes/spec.json`. Scenes over ~25s need extra beats and idle motion (no visual over 30s). Scenes with code reveal it line by line with the narration.
- Sync to the actual spoken idea: use cue text to decide when each visual change appears and disappears. Do not show the next fact early or leave the previous fact onscreen after the voice has moved on. Vary visual grammar between scenes; do not make pipelines/checklists the default when a comparison, spatial model, analogy, chart, capture, or demo would explain better. Keep the mascot's body and position settled through most of a scene; lip-sync should be clearly visible and responsive to speech. Reposition at scene changes or meaningful transitions, with no timer-driven hopping or wandering.
- Scene length = audio length (ffprobe) + 0.4s. `self.wait()` after every animation; one palette; vary entry animations; opacity layers 1.0/0.4/0.15.
- Draft `-ql`, final `-qh --fps 30`. Cache per scene by hash of JSON + audio + kit source (flags like `SUBTITLES` live in `common.py`). Re-render only changed scenes, except after a kit change: then re-render ALL and check each mp4 is newer than the kit. Finals go to `scenes/final/NN.mp4` (1080p, 30fps, H.264 yuv420p CRF 17, silent), drafts stay in `scenes/`.
- On errors read only the last 30 lines of the log. Original vector art only; brand marks may appear as part of a relevant, accurate product screenshot, not as decorative branding.
- Mascot and text: she never covers text; sweep her path across the whole scene (a frame every ~3s), not one frame. Check labels, axes and notes for overlaps; dimmed text must stay legible. Show only claims present in the narration/`plan.json` `claims`; never show unverified quotes.
- `plan.json` `narration` is spoken copy; `on_screen_copy` is visual-only. Preserve exact spoken facts and numbers; never feed visual jokes into TTS, cues, or subtitles. Keep every visual gag brief and subordinate to the point.
- Check at least one full-size frame per scene and say which frames you did NOT view.
- Reply in under 60 words: clips rendered, clips failed, paths.
- If given a `preferences.json` path, read it first and follow it (tone, voice, visuals, topics); it overrides these defaults except legal/safety rules.
